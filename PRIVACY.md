# StreamXR privacy policy

*Last updated: November 6, 2026 (StreamXR 1.0.0)*

StreamXR is a free, open-source browser extension that lets you watch YouTube in a VR headset.
It doesn't collect, store, sell or share any of your data. There are no StreamXR servers, no
analytics and no tracking.

## What StreamXR can access

- **YouTube pages only** (`www.youtube.com`, the extension's one permission). It has no access
  to other sites, your other tabs or your browsing history.
- **What YouTube already shows you:** to draw YouTube in VR, StreamXR reads the YouTube page
  you have open (the video, titles, comments, your subscriptions and playlists) and, when you
  ask it to in VR, does what YouTube's own buttons do (subscribe, like, comment, save to a
  playlist, search). These requests go **only to YouTube**, as you, the same way YouTube's
  own website sends them. Nothing goes anywhere else.

## What stays on your computer

- **Your settings** (panel layout, colors, passthrough and so on) are saved in your browser
  and never leave it.
- **The StreamXR Dashboard and the toolbar popup** check your headset right on your computer.
  They show each site's own icon, loaded from that site.
- **Copy details** (troubleshooting info for bug reports) is copied to your clipboard only
  when you click it: your browser and operating system versions, your graphics card, whether
  a headset is found, headset and video-format info, and StreamXR's own recent warnings (with
  video, playlist and channel IDs removed). It never includes what you watch or your account,
  and it never leaves your computer unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

## The optional Skip Helper

The Skip Helper is a separate, optional extension (not part of StreamXR itself) that presses
YouTube's Skip ad button for you in VR. It works only in YouTube tabs, collects nothing, and
sends nothing anywhere.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here, with its date. Questions?
Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/FauxxYT/StreamXR/issues). The code is open source,
so you can check everything above yourself.

StreamXR is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube, Google or Valve.
